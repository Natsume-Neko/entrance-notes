1. Laplace Transform
	Laplace Transform is an integral transform that converts a function of a real variable (usually $t$, in the time domain), into a function of complex variable $s$ (in the complex-valued frequency domain). It is defined as following: $\mathcal{L}\{f(t)\}= \int _{0}^{\infty} e^{-st}f(t)dt$. The transform can be used as a tool for solving linear differential equations and dynamic systems by simplifying ordinary differential equations and integral equations into algebraic polynomial equations, and by simplifying convolution into multiplication. And the inverse Laplace Transform is used to convert back from $s$ domain to $t$ domain. It has some good properties such as linearity, shifting in time, differentiation in time and integration in time.
