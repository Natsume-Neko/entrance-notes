Divide and Conquer is a family of algorithm that divides the original problem into a set of smaller subproblems, solves the subproblems recursively and combines them to form the solution of original problem. The steps of Divide and Conquer contain: 1. Divide the original problem into a number of subproblems that are smaller instances of original problem; 2. Conquer the subproblems by solving them recursively. If they are small enough, solve them directly; 3. Combine the solutions of the subproblems into the solution of original problem. Divide and Conquer approach reduces problem size of each step, which can lead to better efficiency. An example is merge sort, which sorts the array by dividing it into two halves, sorting each half recursively, and merging the sorted halves.
