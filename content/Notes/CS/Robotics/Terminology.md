1. Inverse Kinematics
	Inverse Kinematics is a mathematic method used in Robotics, Computer Graphics and other related field. It computes the angles or positions of joints required for a limb or other articulated structures to reach a specific position in place. For example, in a 2D robotic arm with 2 joints, we can use inverse kinematics to calculate the angles of these 2 joints given the final position of the end effector. To perform the algorithm, we can use the inverse of the Jacobian Matrix or other numeric methods. In the case of Jacobian Inverse, the equation can be $\theta = J^{-1}x$. It is the inverse process of Forward Kinematics.
